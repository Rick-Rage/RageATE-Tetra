SPATIAL_CYCLES         10  RW  16 216
  0 16  Unsigned  SpatialSampleCycles SS

; Historically a "spatial cycle" (a.k.a. SS) was the vertical
; location down the mast where a logical antenna (which is really
; a pairing of a particular Tx and a particular Rx antenna) sampled
; the radar reflection. Hence historically the value in the
; SPATIAL_CYCLES reg could be used as the number of logical antennas.
; But the Mercury AMs have 4 radio demodulators which can be
; simultaneously active. For the operating regime described by
; this FPGA register INI file we simultaneously activate 2 demods
; whenever possible. We can simultaneously activate 2 demods
; for most of the scanline except for 3 logical antennas at
; the top of the mast and 3 logical antennas at the bottom 
; of the mast. Because we can simultaneously activate 2 demods
; during the rest of the time we need 216 chirps to sample
; 426 logical antennas. Note that 216 is NOT half of 426 !!

; Because this SPATIAL_CYCLES reg is used in scan_controller.v
; and trigger_generator.v we need to set it to 216, the # of chirp
; intervals.  But this causes a problem because the value in the
; SPATIAL_CYCLES register has historically been interpeted as
; being the number of "logical antennas" or "antenna pairs".

; In the wideband product we have 426 pairs of Tx/Rx antennas
; and we chirp 216 times during each scanline. AM1 holds the
; active Tx antenna during the first 24 of these chirps, AM2 through AM7
; hold the Tx antenna during 24 chirps each, and AM9 holds the active
; Tx antenna during the final 24 chirps. This adds up to
;  24 + ( 7 * 24 ) + 24 = 216 which is half of 432 NOT half of 426 !!

; During the first  3 chirps AM1 samples           3 logical antennas
; During the next  18 chirps AM1 samples 2 * 18 = 36 logical antennas
; During the next   6 chirps AM1 samples           6 logical antennas
;                        and AM2 samples           6 logical antennas
; During the next  18 chirps AM2 samples 2 * 18 = 36 logical antennas
; During the next   6 chirps AM2 samples           6 logical antennas
;                        and AM3 samples           6 logical antennas
; During the next  18 chirps AM3 samples 2 * 18 = 36 logical antennas
; During the next   6 chirps AM3 samples           6 logical antennas
;                        and AM4 samples           6 logical antennas
; During the next  18 chirps AM4 samples 2 * 18 = 36 logical antennas
; During the next   6 chirps AM4 samples           6 logical antennas
;                        and AM5 samples           6 logical antennas
; During the next  18 chirps AM5 samples 2 * 18 = 36 logical antennas
; During the next   6 chirps AM5 samples           6 logical antennas
;                        and AM6 samples           6 logical antennas
; During the next  18 chirps AM6 samples 2 * 18 = 36 logical antennas
; During the next   6 chirps AM6 samples           6 logical antennas
;                        and AM7 samples           6 logical antennas
; During the next  18 chirps AM7 samples 2 * 18 = 36 logical antennas
; During the next   6 chirps AM7 samples           6 logical antennas
;                        and AM8 samples           6 logical antennas
; During the next  18 chirps AM8 samples 2 * 18 = 36 logical antennas
; During the next   6 chirps AM8 samples           6 logical antennas
;                        and AM9 samples           6 logical antennas
; During the next  18 chirps AM9 samples 2 * 18 = 36 logical antennas
; During the next   3 chirps AM9 samples           3 logical antennas
;              -------------                    ---------------------
;     totaling to 216 chirps         totaling to 426 logical antennas
